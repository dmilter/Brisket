How the numbers work. Tenderness uses the Smoke Trails rendering table: each internal temperature adds a percent per hour (1% at 140°F up to 75% at 210°F), and the page adds up time at each temperature between your readings, including the cooldown and the hold. The projection carries your recent heating rate to the pull, then an estimated cooldown. It is an estimate, and brisket varies.

Finish times are ranges, not promises. Through the stall (about 150 to 180°F) the range is wide on purpose. Probe feel overrules every number here.
