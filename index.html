Overnight gap? Copy the probe history from your Traeger or Smokecraft graph. One reading per line, oldest first, like 11:30 pm 148 or 23:30, 148.

How the numbers work. Tenderness uses the Smoke Trails rendering table: each internal temperature adds a percent per hour (1% at 140°F up to 75% at 210°F), and the page sums time at each temperature, including the cooldown and the hold. It is an estimate, and brisket varies.

Finish times are ranges, not promises. Through the stall (about 150 to 180°F) the range is wide on purpose. Probe feel overrules every number here.
